This file exists so the writing templates have something to render. Replace it.

The `description` in the frontmatter is what shows on the index and in the RSS
feed, so it should say what the piece argues rather than tease it.

## A section heading

Body copy is EB Garamond at 20px. Headings, blockquotes, lists, `inline code`,
and code blocks are all styled in `src/styles/global.css` under `.prose`.

> A pull quote sits in the margin-free style: a hairline rule, italic, and set
> in the muted text colour.

Three kinds are available in the frontmatter — `essay`, `review`, and `note` —
and the kind is what a reader sees above the title.
